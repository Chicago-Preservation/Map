<h2>Neighborhood Walking Tours</h2>

<p>
  These walking tours explore Chicago neighborhoods through the lens of historic preservation — examining buildings, businesses, parks, and institutions that embody layers of immigration, labor, urban change, and community memory. Each tour traces a route through a specific neighborhood, documenting sites that reveal how successive generations have built upon, adapted, and sometimes erased the urban fabric they inherited.
</p>

<p>
  Toggle the <strong>Walking Tours</strong> layer in the map legend to explore the routes spatially. Click any numbered stop to read about that site, or click the route line to open the full tour in the sidebar.
</p>

<h3>Current Tours</h3>
<p>
  The first set of walking tours was produced by students in <em>Introduction to Historic Preservation</em>, taught by Professor Emily Talen at the University of Chicago in Fall 2025.
</p>
