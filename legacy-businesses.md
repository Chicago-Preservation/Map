<h2>Chicago Legacy Businesses</h2>

<p>
  This layer maps Chicago businesses that have operated continuously for decades, serving as anchors of neighborhood identity, culture, and community memory. Each pin links to a research post examining the business's history, its role in the surrounding neighborhood, and the preservation challenges it faces in an era of rapid urban change.
</p>

<p>
  Toggle the <strong>Legacy Business</strong> layer in the map legend to explore the sites spatially. Click any pin to read the associated research post in the sidebar.
</p>

<h3>Current Projects</h3>
<p>
  The first set of legacy business profiles was produced by students in <em>Public History</em>, taught by Dr. Melissa Rovner at Lake Forest College in Spring 2026.
</p>
